About Us

Daily Quiz Answers is an independent website that publishes general knowledge, trivia and exam quiz guides for readers in Pakistan. Our guides cover Pakistan general knowledge, history, geography, science, mathematics, English vocabulary, current affairs, sports, Islamic studies and exam preparation for CSS, NTS and PPSC.

We also build free in-browser practice tools — a GK quiz, a trivia generator, a timed exam quiz, a score tracker and a random question generator — so readers can test themselves without signing up or downloading anything.

We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to any quiz app, television channel, examination body or mobile operator. Quiz answers and rewards change daily; we explain how to find and verify answers rather than publishing leaked daily answers.

Our goal is simple: honest, accurate, practical quiz help. Every fact in our guides is checked against reliable sources, and anything time-sensitive — such as exam dates or app rewards — is labelled so you know to check the official source.
